## 1. About this policy

JobBridge is an Android app for finding work and hiring people in Uzbekistan.
This policy explains how we collect, use, store and protect JobBridge user data.

A user may act as a candidate, employer or, if they work for the operator, an
administrator. We do not sell personal data or provide it for advertising. The
app has no advertising or behavioural analytics SDK.

## 2. Data we collect

### Account and sign-in

- phone number;
- a hash of the one-time sign-in code;
- selected interface language;
- account status and status history;
- device identifier and name, platform and app version;
- sessions, token hashes, creation time and last-use time;
- the IP address of a sign-in attempt and rate-limit records.

The phone number identifies the account and is used to sign in with an SMS
code. The code expires after five minutes and is not stored in clear text.

### Candidate profile

A candidate may provide:

- full name, date of birth and gender;
- region, district and settlement;
- occupations, skills, qualifications and languages;
- employment type, work format, shifts and expected pay;
- work history and education;
- depending on the work, driving licences, transport, tools, readiness and
  specialisation;
- profile photo, CV and certificates;
- portfolio link and available-from date;
- profile visibility and completeness percentage.

We do not read location from the device. The user enters their location. CVs
are not parsed automatically; search uses the profile fields.

### Employer profile

An employer may provide:

- company or individual type;
- contact name and phone number;
- region, address and description;
- company legal and public names, industry and logo;
- company-registration document;
- identity document for a representative or individual employer;
- verification status and history.

Only authorised administrators can review verification documents. Every
administrator download is logged.

### Data created while using the app

We store vacancies, applications and their stages, invitations, interviews,
messages and attachments, complaints, saved vacancies and candidates,
shortlists and blocks.

We also record an employer's Coin balance, Coin credits and debits, and which
candidate's contact details were unlocked. Buying Coins is not currently
available. If payments launch, Payme and/or CLICK will process them. Bank-card
details will not reach JobBridge.

### Notifications and technical records

We store in-app notifications and user preferences. For push notifications, a
device token, notification title and text are sent through Google Firebase
Cloud Messaging. Documents are not included in push notifications.

Server logs may contain request metadata and an IP address. Only the last two
digits of a phone number are shown, and one-time codes are never logged.

## 3. Data we do not collect

JobBridge does not read device location, contacts, calendar, call logs or SMS.
The user selects photos and documents through Android's system file picker.

## 4. Why we use data

We use data to:

- create accounts and sign users in;
- provide candidate and employer profiles;
- search for vacancies and candidates;
- process applications, interviews and messages;
- verify employers and moderate content;
- review complaints;
- send notifications;
- secure the service and prevent abuse;
- maintain the Coin ledger.

The match indicator uses profile fields such as occupation, skills, region and
pay. An employer or administrator, not an automated system, makes the final
decision about hiring, rejection or account moderation.

## 5. Who can see data

A candidate controls profile visibility. A hidden profile is not visible to
employers. Search cards do not show the candidate's phone number, email or CV.
Submitting an application does not unlock that information. An employer sees
it only after spending Coins to unlock that specific candidate.

An employer sees its own vacancies and applications to them. A candidate and
employer see the messages and files exchanged in their conversation.

Administrators can see accounts, verification documents, vacancies awaiting
moderation and reported content. They cannot browse private conversations that
have not been reported.

## 6. Service providers

JobBridge uses the following services:

- **the operator's server:** the main database and backups;
- **Telegram Bot API:** storage of uploaded CVs, photos, certificates,
  verification documents and message attachments;
- **Google Firebase Cloud Messaging:** push tokens and notifications;
- **Eskiz.uz:** delivery of the sign-in code to the phone number;
- **Cloudflare:** protection and forwarding of HTTPS traffic;
- **Payme and/or CLICK:** payment processing after Coin purchases launch.

Telegram and Google servers may be outside Uzbekistan. Files are downloaded
through JobBridge; users do not receive Telegram links. These services process
data so JobBridge can operate. We do not provide data for advertising.

We may provide data when required by applicable law, a court, or an authorised
public body, but only to the extent permitted by law.

## 7. Retention

- A 30-day waiting period applies after an account-deletion request.
- After that period, the phone number, profile, applications, messages,
  sessions and other active data linked to the person are deleted.
- Administrator audit records and the Coin financial ledger may be kept
  permanently. The user's name, phone number and other direct identifiers are
  removed from retained records.
- One-time codes are kept for 1 day, unused sessions for 90 days, rate-limit
  records for 2 days and in-app notifications for 180 days.
- Database backups are kept for 14 days.

When an account is deleted, JobBridge removes the record that locates a file,
so the file can no longer be accessed through the app. Telegram may not allow
some files older than 48 hours to be deleted automatically; those files may
remain in storage until the operator removes them manually. Deleted data may remain in a backup until its
14-day retention period expires.

## 8. User choices

Users can view and edit their profile, remove uploaded files, change language,
revoke device sessions, block another user and report content.

To delete an account, go to **Profile → Account → Delete account**. If you
cannot access the app, email [CONTACT EMAIL]. More information is available on
the [account-deletion page](https://hh.qitmir.uz/account/delete).

To ask about your data, request a correction or ask for processing to be
suspended, email [CONTACT EMAIL].

## 9. Age

A user must be at least 14 years old to create a candidate profile. Minors must
use the app in accordance with applicable law.

## 10. Security

All traffic is encrypted with HTTPS. Sign-in codes and tokens are not stored in
clear text. Phone numbers are masked in logs. Sign-in and file-upload rates are
limited. File types are validated, and only entitled users can download them.
The database is backed up nightly.
